# Bookkeeping Validation Sprint — 2026-10-05

Status: **ACTIVE — first batch sent 2026-10-05**

## Hypothesis

For bookkeeping/accounting firms with meaningful recurring-client volume, missing client documents and unanswered transaction questions still create manual follow-up work and/or delay month-end close even when the firm already uses QBO/Xero, Dext/Hubdoc, Karbon, Financial Cents, a CRM, or a client portal.

This sprint tests the **residual pain after normal tooling**, not whether client reminders exist.

## Secondary discovery

Do not broaden the initial pitch. After a reply or on a call, ask:

> Besides chasing clients, what is the most manual part between receiving client documents and having the books ready to close?

Track adjacent pain separately: intake, extraction, categorization, reconciliation, exception handling, document matching, approvals, reporting.

## Success criteria

Within 15–25 highly qualified prospects:

- >=3 substantive pain confirmations;
- >=2 prospects quantify time, follow-up touches, close delay, or staff cost;
- >=1 behavioral commitment: workflow walkthrough, redacted sample/status list, call, or concierge test;
- paid pilot is stronger evidence but not required for Day 4.

## Stop / pivot

Do not add another 50 generic firms if the first qualified batch is weak.

- 0 substantive replies after first 15 + one follow-up: change ICP/contact role/framing/channel.
- Firms consistently report portals/reminders remove the pain: pivot from chasing to the adjacent pre-close bottleneck they name.
- 20–25 high-fit prospects with weak pain and no test behavior: Archive or substantially reformulate.

## Privacy boundary for concierge test

Do **not** ask for confidential client financial documents in the first pilot.

Start with:
- redacted/anonymized outstanding-item descriptions;
- dummy/synthetic examples;
- metadata only (client alias, requested item, due date, status, last follow-up);
- no bank credentials, tax IDs, financial statements, payroll data, or personally identifying client documents.

Only expand access after explicit permissions and an appropriate secure handling process.

## 1. Last real close
“Thinking about your most recent monthly close, how many client accounts were waiting on documents or answers?”

## 2. Volume
“How many recurring monthly clients does the team handle, roughly?”

## 3. Current stack
“What handles client requests today — Karbon, Financial Cents, Dext/Hubdoc, email, WhatsApp, CRM, something else?”

## 4. Automation boundary
“What happens automatically, and at what point does a person step in?”

## 5. Follow-up burden
“For the clients who need human chasing, how many touches is typical?”

## 6. Ownership
“Who actually does that work — bookkeeper, account manager, admin, partner?”

## 7. Cost
“Across a month, roughly how much staff time does this consume?”

## 8. Close delay
“Does waiting for clients actually move the close date, or is it mainly annoying admin?”

## 9. Current workaround
“How do you keep track of unresolved items across clients?”

## 10. Existing-tool gap
“What does your current practice-management/client portal still not do well enough?”

## 11. Adjacent discovery
“Besides chasing clients, what is the most manual part between receiving client documents and having the books ready to close?”

## 12. Behavior test
“If I ran a small one-close experiment around 5–10 client accounts without replacing your current tools, would you be willing to try it?”

# Concierge offer

## Framing

Do not propose a full SaaS.

Offer:

> For one close cycle, I’ll help manage the outstanding-item layer for 5–10 client accounts on top of your existing tools. We’ll keep a simple status list, identify what is still blocking close, and prepare follow-up drafts for the exceptions that need a person. We’ll measure how many touches happen, how much staff time it consumes, and whether the items are resolved faster. You keep the current QBO/Xero/Karbon/Financial Cents workflow.

## Initial data boundary

Use client aliases / IDs only.

Suggested fields:
- Client alias
- Outstanding item / question
- Requested date
- Last reminder date
- Current status
- Close blocker? yes/no
- Owner
- Next action

No raw financial documents required for the first test.

## Pilot success measure

Compare:
- human follow-up touches;
- time spent tracking/chasing;
- unresolved items after N days;
- days between request and resolution;
- close blockers remaining at the cutoff.

## Commitment ladder

1. reply with workflow;
2. quantify last close;
3. 15-minute walkthrough;
4. share redacted outstanding-item structure;
5. accept one-close concierge test;
6. agree to repeat;
7. discuss paid pilot / monthly price.

Do not count “sounds interesting” as validation.
